FAQ

What poster sizes are available?

A6 is ₹19, A5 is ₹29, A4 is ₹45, and A3 is ₹79 per poster. Custom sizes are available with a quotation.

What Polaroid sizes are available?

2.5 × 3 inches costs ₹15, 3 × 4 inches costs ₹20, and 6 × 6 inches costs ₹25 per print. Custom sizes are available with a quotation.

How much is delivery?

Delivery costs ₹50 within Tamil Nadu. Delivery outside Tamil Nadu costs ₹80, including the additional ₹30 charge.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

No. Studio Poster accepts prepaid orders only.

How do I send my photos?

Select your photos in the order form. They are only previewed in your browser, so attach the original image files to your WhatsApp order message.

Can I customize the design?

Yes. Specify custom sizes and your preferred colors, text, and design instructions. Final custom pricing is confirmed by the business.

How is my order confirmed?

After you submit your order through WhatsApp, Studio Poster will review the details and provide confirmation and payment instructions.
